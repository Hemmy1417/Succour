Eastfield Provincial Hazards Agency

Lower Marrow flood bulletin

A flood warning is in force for the Lower Marrow river basin in Eastfield province.

The flooding began on 24 September 2026 after three days of continuous rainfall.

River gauges across the basin stand above the warning level of 4.2 metres this morning.

The gauge at Marrow Bridge has passed the danger level of 5.9 metres and an evacuation of the lower town has been ordered.

Published by Eastfield Provincial Hazards Agency as a public bulletin.
