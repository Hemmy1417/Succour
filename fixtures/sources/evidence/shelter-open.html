Eastfield Provincial Hazards Agency

Eastfield municipal shelter status

The site assessed is Riverside Row in the lower town of Eastfield, inside the Lower Marrow river basin.

The municipal shelter at this site is open, dry and operating below capacity; no household here is without accommodation.

The damage recorded here was caused by the flooding that began on 24 September 2026.

Survey completed on 25 September 2026 by the Eastfield district relief office.

Published by Eastfield Provincial Hazards Agency as a public field assessment.
